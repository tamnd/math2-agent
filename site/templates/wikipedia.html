Wikipedia scope

The corpus of Math 2.0 takes the mathematics articles of the English Wikipedia. This page shows which articles are in scope, and the signal or the rule that put each one there. The text of the articles is not on this site. Each title links to the article on Wikipedia.

A candidate is an article with one or more of the signals S1 to S9 of the spec (WP1.2). The rules decide the candidates where their precision is high. The scope model pass decides the other candidates, and until then they are not decided.
