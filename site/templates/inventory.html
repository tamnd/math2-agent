Inventory

The inventory is the list of each item of mathematics that a source presents: each definition, theorem, conjecture, problem and object. Each row is one item. It groups the mentions of the item in the sources. A row is open until math2 decides what to do with it, for example to make a node for it. The open count is the number of open rows, and the target is zero.
