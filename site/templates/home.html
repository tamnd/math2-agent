How Math 2.0 works

Small pages

Each page is one idea: a definition, a theorem, an example. Pages link to the ideas they build on, so you can always step back to what you need.

Plain words first

Next to the exact statement, most pages explain the idea in plain words, with a picture you can hold in your head.

Open to all

The text is free to share and change. Anyone can help write it, and agents can read the same content as data.
