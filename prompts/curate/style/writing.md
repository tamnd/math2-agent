# Writing conventions of math2

- State the result in your own words. Do not copy the sentences of the record, even when its licence lets math2 store them.
- Write plain English with short sentences.
- Use LaTeX in $...$ for formulas, as the node files do.
- Write a link as [[text|TAG]], where TAG is the tag of a node, for example [[a group|G5S1M9]]. Do not link to a URL.
- Put a blank line before and after each heading and each paragraph.
- Do not use em dashes.
