# Writing conventions of the results

- Use the kinds of the list exactly, for example statement/lemma or definition/notation.
- Give a confidence with two decimals, for example 0.85.
- Give the common name of a concept in the singular and in lower case, for example "moduli space of stable curves", other than a proper name such as "Riemann surface".
- Give a Wikidata item as the QID only, for example Q193297.
- Give a symbol as the paper writes it in LaTeX, for example \mathcal{M}_g.
- Do not use em dashes.
