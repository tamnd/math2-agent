# The pass summary, version 1

The pass summary gives each statement and definition a summary of one sentence in plain words, for search and as a hint for a write job. "Every compact metric space is complete" is a good summary of a theorem; "Theorem 2 holds for compact spaces" is not, because it names an item by its number.

Say what the item states or defines, with its main objects and its conclusion, so that a reader who searches for it finds it. Use words in place of notation where you can. Keep each summary to one sentence of at most 300 characters. Do not add what the item does not say.

Give the results with give_summaries, all the items in one call.
