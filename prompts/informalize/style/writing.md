# The house style of math2

- Write plain English. A sentence of the main section has at most 25 words, and a sentence of another section at most 30. A formula counts as one word.
- Do not write "clearly", "obviously", "trivially", "it is easy to see" or "the reader can verify". Give the step instead.
- Do not use "we" in the Statement and Definition sections.
- Do not refer to a place, such as "Section 3" or "the previous chapter". Link to the node.
- Use LaTeX in $...$ for formulas, with only the macros of the register.
- Write a link as [[text|TAG]], where TAG is the tag of a node, for example [[a group|G5S1M9]]. Do not put a URL in the Statement, Definition or Problem sections.
- Put a blank line before and after each heading and each paragraph.
- Do not use em dashes.
